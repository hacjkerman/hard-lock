Hard Lock needs
Screen Time
It is the only way iOS lets an app block other apps. Nothing leaves your phone — no account, no network, no usage data.
A cutoff each night
Set a time per weekday. After it, every app is shielded until 4 am.
Tighten now, loosen later
Making a rule stricter is instant. Relaxing one waits 24 hours.
Deleting the app ends it
This is strong friction, not security. A Screen Time passcode closes that door.
