Hard Lock Setup
Step 1 of 5 · Welcome

A lock you set for yourself.

Hard Lock shuts your computer down when you hit a daily limit or a nightly cutoff you choose — while you're rested enough to choose well.

Honest up front: this is friction, not security. Anyone determined can end the process or remove the task. It's built to make the easy path the one you actually wanted.
Step 2 of 5 · How it works

Two limits, one rule.

Daily active cap

Counts time you're actually at the keyboard. Idle time doesn't count. When it runs out, the machine shuts down.

Hard cutoff

A wall-clock time after which the machine won't stay on — no matter how much cap you have left.

Tighten now, loosen later. Making the lock stricter takes effect immediately. Making it looser (raising the cap, pushing the cutoff later) waits out a cooldown — so a tired 1 a.m. you can't undo what rested you decided.

Step 4 of 5 · Arm it

Try it, or arm it.

Dry-run mode
Simulates the shutdown instead of performing it — safe to try. Turn off to make it real.
Start at logon
Runs Hard Lock automatically when you sign in (installs a Task Scheduler task — needs administrator once).
Lock in a commitment
Commit the lock for a fixed term. Until it ends you can't disarm or weaken it — only tighten. Off by default.
Step 5 of 5 · You're set

That's the decision.
