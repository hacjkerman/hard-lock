Limits

Whichever comes first shuts down your computer. Tightening applies immediately. Loosening waits out your cooldown.

Per-day limits
A different daily cap and hard cutoff for each day — today is highlighted. Lowering a cap or setting an earlier cutoff applies now; raising a cap or a later cutoff waits out your cooldown. Blank cutoff = none that day. A cutoff after midnight (e.g. 01:30) belongs to that night — a Friday 01:30 keeps you up until 1:30 Sat morning.
